2. Personal Data We Collect

We keep data collection at the company-website level deliberately minimal:

  • Contact/inquiry form data: if you reach out to us through a contact or inquiry form on the Site, we collect what you provide; typically your name, email address, company name, and the content of your message.
  • Email correspondence: if you email us directly (e.g. at info@digital360.com.sg), we retain that correspondence and any information you choose to include in it.
  • Automatically collected data: standard website analytics such as IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, and referring site, collected via cookies and similar technologies (see Section 6).

We do not collect payment information, government ID numbers, or other sensitive data through the Site itself.

3. How We Use Your Personal Data

We use the personal data described above to:

  • Respond to your inquiry or message.
  • Understand who is visiting the Site and how, so we can improve it.
  • Maintain records of business correspondence for our own reference.
  • Comply with applicable law, where relevant.

We do not use contact-form or correspondence data for unsolicited marketing without your consent, and we do not sell personal data to third parties.

We share personal data collected through the Site only with:
